# Prompt de video — recorrido del tráiler a la lata

Create a single continuous 8-second cinematic camera move for a premium mobile coffee brand website, landscape 16:9, designed to be scrubbed forward and backward by page scroll.

Start outside a very clean compact white mobile coffee trailer parked at a sunny community event lot in Hilton Head / Bluffton, South Carolina. The trailer has warm medium-brown wood service panels and counter, open white awnings, restrained blue accents, and a cream handmade square-tile interior. This is a modern mobile coffee operation, not a coastal resort, beach café, rustic cabin, or indoor coffee shop.

The camera slowly dollies toward the open service window, passes the clean espresso station, then glides down toward the wood counter. End in a precise product close-up of a tall transparent PET beverage can filled with a layered iced brown-sugar latte, visible ice and condensation, sealed with a flat silver aluminum pull-tab lid. The container must unmistakably be a sealed can—never a cup, mug, glass, jar, bottle, or tumbler. The final can stays perfectly still for the last 1.5 seconds so website text can appear beside it.

Visual style: polished natural editorial hospitality film, realistic trailer geometry and machinery, crisp late-morning daylight, white, cream, walnut brown, charcoal and small cornflower-blue accents, subtle 35mm film texture, shallow depth of field near the final drink, smooth stabilized camera, realistic reflections and condensation.

No people, no hands, no readable text, no invented logos, no watermark, no ocean, no palm-tree emphasis, no tropical décor, no jump cuts, no camera shake, no warped wheels or equipment. Keep exposure and geometry consistent across every frame. First and last frames should hold for clean scroll interpolation.

Suggested export: 3840×2160 or 1920×1080, 24 fps, 8 seconds, MP4 H.264 plus WebM fallback if available, no audio.
